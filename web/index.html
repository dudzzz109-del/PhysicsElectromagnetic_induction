How it works

A changing magnetic flux through the coil induces an EMF: EMF = −N dΦ/dt (Faraday). The induced current opposes the change in flux (Lenz). In PASS THROUGH mode the magnet goes in one side of the coil and out the other: the field through the coil reverses when the N pole crosses it, so the flux changes sign and the current reverses. BOUNCE mode: the magnet goes in and comes back out the same side.

Current direction is given as seen from the right (the side the magnet starts on). Needle: left = negative current, right = positive.
